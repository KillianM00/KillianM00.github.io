Independent security research / responsible disclosure

Anthropic bug bounty research

Investigating how repository-controlled configuration interacts with workspace trust and execution permissions in developer tools.

Research question → controlled reproduction → evidence → vendor review

Recognized by Anthropic

In May 2026, Anthropic recognized my submission as informative and said it aided the discovery of other vulnerabilities.

The research question

Developer tools can load configuration from a repository while also offering permission settings intended to limit execution. My research examined how repository-provided hooks and MCP tool-server configuration interact with workspace trust and noninteractive execution in Anthropic’s coding-agent CLI.

The engineering question was where those controls take effect during startup, and how observed behavior changes across execution modes and permission settings.

My contribution

  • Reproduction cases: prepared concrete scenarios for examining repository configuration and execution behavior.
  • Permission-mode comparisons: compared behavior across configurations to test the boundaries of the initial hypothesis.
  • Startup timing evidence: documented when relevant behavior occurred during initialization and execution.
  • Responsible disclosure: submitted the research to Anthropic and followed up with its security team to examine alternative explanations.

Technical skills demonstrated

Threat modeling: reasoning about the boundary between untrusted repository content and a tool operating with the user’s permissions.

Experimental design: using reproduction cases, configuration comparisons, and timing evidence to distinguish an observation from its proposed explanation.

Security communication: presenting a technical hypothesis with supporting evidence, responding to vendor feedback, and refining the interpretation through follow-up.

Disclosure outcome

Anthropic’s feedback recognized the submission as informative and helpful in discovering other vulnerabilities. This public summary presents the research process and that acknowledgment; the detailed report and correspondence remain private.

The experience reinforced the value of a precise reproduction and careful treatment of competing explanations during security review.
